FAQ

What tools do you use?

Google Workspace and Microsoft 365 for email, calendars and documents; Trello, Notion, Asana or ClickUp for project tracking; and Zapier, Make or custom scripts for automation. I'm happy to work in the tools you already use.

How do you keep my information private?

I treat everything you share as confidential, access only what a task needs, and use strong passwords and two-factor authentication. I'm also happy to sign an NDA.

Can you automate workflows?

Yes. Repetitive tasks such as form-to-spreadsheet entries, reminders and email sorting can often be automated with Zapier, Make or simple scripts.
