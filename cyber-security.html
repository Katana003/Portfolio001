5 Habits That Block Most Attacks

You don't need a big budget to be safer. Start here.

🔑

Unique passwords

Use a password manager so every account has its own strong password.

📲

Two-factor login

Turn on 2FA everywhere it is offered, especially email and banking.

🎣

Think before you click

Pause on urgent emails, unexpected attachments and links you didn't ask for.

🔄

Keep things updated

Install updates for your phone, laptop and apps; many fix known security holes.

💾

Back up your data

Keep copies in a separate place so one mistake or attack can't wipe you out.

FAQ

Why does a small startup need cyber security?

Attackers often target small businesses because they assume the defences are weaker. Basic habits such as strong passwords, 2FA, updates and backups stop many common attacks.

How often should my team get security training?

Training works best as a regular refresher rather than a one-off, and whenever a new type of scam or threat starts affecting businesses like yours.

Do you offer penetration testing?

Not at the moment. My focus is awareness, guidance, research and secure development. If you need specialised testing, I can point you to qualified professionals.

Can you work with remote teams?

Yes. Training, consultations and guidance can all be done online.
